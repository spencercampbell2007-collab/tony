# Use Referral Links Before Affiliate Programs

For a US beginner with about 38 real contacts, **the best-paying "program" is usually not an affiliate program at all. It is the plain refer-a-friend (RAF) link built into each app.** RAF links need no approval, no website and no payout threshold. Per head, they often pay as much as or more than the affiliate CPA (cost per acquisition) a beginner would be offered. Chime's referral reportedly pays **$100 to you and $100 to your friend**, against a reported $8–$75 affiliate CPA. Rakuten officially pays **$50 per referral**, against $25–$40 in older affiliate listings. The easiest *true* affiliate programs to join are **Polymarket's Dub-powered program** (rolling, case-by-case approval), **Kraken** (reportedly open to anyone, but paid as revenue share), and the **FlexOffers/Impact** networks for Robinhood, Moomoo and Acorns, which are much easier to get into once you have a simple one-page website. DFS creator programs (PrizePicks, Underdog, ParlayPlay) are a realistic second step once your Discord has a track record. Major sportsbooks (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, bet365) are the hardest. They review applicants by hand, require compliance pre-approval, and in several states require you to register as a vendor, so a group-chat-only applicant will likely be rejected. Four rules are non-negotiable: **don't self-refer or run friends' accounts, don't pay friends to sign up, don't promote sweepstakes casinos, and put a clear "#ad" disclosure plus 21+ and 1-800-GAMBLER language on every gambling post.**

> **Read this first.** Every web page the researchers tried to open failed with DNS errors. **Every figure in this report comes from search-result snippets**, mostly from third-party affiliate directories, vendor blogs and promo-code sites. These often conflict and carry stale data. No operator's official affiliate terms or regulator fee schedule was read in full. Treat every payout, cap, fee and state list as *reported, not verified*, and confirm it on the official terms page or inside the network dashboard before you rely on it. The legal and tax material describes what sources reported. **It is not legal or tax advice.** Ask a gaming attorney before registering in any state, and a CPA about taxes.

The logic is that **breadth beats depth**. A friend can open Chime, sign up for Rakuten and try Kalshi, and each of those is a separate referral. Your earnings scale with how many apps each friend is willing to try, not with the per-app rate on an affiliate contract you probably can't get yet.

## Thirty-eight friends earn more through refer-a-friend than CPA

Affiliate CPAs look larger in headlines, but a beginner without traffic rarely gets the top rate. Many fintech affiliate figures are "up to" amounts tied to the friend completing a direct deposit or buying a specific product. The research found that **RAF beats or matches affiliate CPA for Chime, SoFi and Rakuten**. Affiliate wins mainly where the RAF is weak, such as Robinhood (historically about $10 each side vs. a $16–$20 CPA) and probably Moomoo ($40 CPA) ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/robinhood-fee-free-brokerage-referral-program-10-users-share-links); [FlexOffers Moomoo](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/moomoo-affiliate-program/)). RAF has a second advantage that matters for persuading friends: **the friend gets a bonus too**, so you aren't asking a favor.

The table below models two uptake levels for each app. "10 complete" is a cautious case, about a quarter of your contacts doing the qualifying step. "25 complete" is optimistic. These are illustrations built from reported rates, not forecasts.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chime | $100 | $1,000 | $2,500 | $8–$75 ([UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-programs/chime/)) | $80–$750 | $200–$1,875 |
| SoFi | $75 | $750 | $1,875 | "up to $100", unverified ([UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-programs/sofi/)) | ≤$1,000 | ≤$2,500 |
| Rakuten | $50 | $500 | $1,250 | $25–$40, older listings | $250–$400 | $625–$1,000 |
| Robinhood | ~$10 stock (historical) | ~$100 | ~$250 | $16–$20 | $160–$200 | $400–$500 |
| Kalshi | $25 *credit*, 7-day expiry | $250 credit | $625 credit | negotiated, no public rate ([gamblingaffiliatehub](https://gamblingaffiliatehub.com/programs/kalshi/)) | n/a | n/a |
| Dabble / PrizePicks | Dabble $30 bonus cash | $300 bonus | $750 bonus | PrizePicks $25–$60+ (if approved) | $250–$600 | $625–$1,500 |
| Polymarket | — | — | — | $10 per $20 depositor (disputed) | ~$100 | ~$250 |

Three caveats keep these numbers honest. First, **caps matter**. Kalshi's referral cap is reported at $1,000 or $2,000 a year, Robinhood's referral rewards are capped at $1,500 per person per year ([Robinhood support](https://robinhood.com/support/articles/invite-friends-get-free-stock)), and caps for Chime, Varo and SoFi weren't found. Second, **gambling RAF money is mostly not cash**. Kalshi and Dabble pay credits you must play through, and Kalshi's expire in seven days. Third, **the fintech RAFs ask real things of friends**: a $200 direct deposit (Chime, Current) or $500 of debit spending (Varo). Expect fewer completions than signups. A realistic blended outcome, say 15 friends each doing Chime, Rakuten and Kalshi, works out to roughly $2,600 of mixed cash and credit (15 × $175). That beats any affiliate deal a 38-person audience can unlock today.

Affiliate programs become worth it later, for two reasons. RAF links can be capped or limited to personal sharing, which leaves no room to grow, while affiliate networks pool many brands into one payout and keep paying as the Discord grows past your friend list. Whether RAF terms forbid posting codes publicly in a Discord was **not researched**. Check each RAF's terms before you pin links in a public channel.

## Polymarket, Kraken and FlexOffers accept beginners; big sportsbooks don't

### Betting, DFS and prediction markets

**No major sportsbook or pick'em operator runs an open, auto-approve program.** FanDuel, Hard Rock and Caesars use Income Access panels, bet365 and ParlayPlay run their own portals, and PrizePicks and Underdog sign direct creator deals ([Partnerkin](https://partnerkin.com/en/b2b/review/income-access/); [StatsDrone](https://statsdrone.com/affiliate-programs/caesars-affiliates/)). DraftKings and BetMGM review applications manually, and marketing materials need state-level compliance approval before going live ([track360 DraftKings](https://track360.io/blog/draftkings-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026); [track360 BetMGM](https://track360.io/blog/betmgm-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026)). Traffic is geo-gated, so out-of-state clicks don't pay. New BetMGM affiliates typically start on CPA only for 30–90 days, with no published rate ([track360 BetMGM](https://track360.io/blog/betmgm-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026)). Reported CPAs for the big books conflict sharply: DraftKings is listed at a flat $250 by one directory and $100–$500 by another, and FanDuel at $25–$35 vs. $100–$500 ([UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/draftkings/); [15m.com](https://15m.com/affiliate-programs/fanduel-affiliate/)). Payment is typically monthly, about Net-30 to Net-45, with $50–$100 minimums ([StatsDrone](https://statsdrone.com/best-affiliate-programs/sports-betting/)).

**Polymarket is the closest thing to an open program.** It launched an official affiliate program on Dub in late November 2025, accepting applicants "on rolling & case by case basis," and thousands of X accounts carry its affiliate badges ([Steven Tey on X](https://x.com/steventey/status/1992678397466804554)). Community guides describe joining as: open a Polymarket account, deposit $3–$20, apply at partners.dub.co/polymarket with the same email, and wait about 72 hours ([polymarket-partners.com guide](https://polymarket-partners.com/guide)). Those guides are unofficial. The payout model is unresolved, and one guide's $10,000 lifetime-volume requirement would rule out a beginner if it's true. It's also unclear whether the Dub program covers the separate **Polymarket US** app, which one source says serves 48 states plus DC, excluding Minnesota and Nevada ([Bettors Insider](https://bettorsinsider.com/predictions/promotions/polymarket/)). Watch for lookalikes: several "join" pages belong to community partners, and "Kalshi Affiliate Program — Earn 20% Forever" belongs to a third-party tool, KalshiSpy ([kalshispy](https://kalshispy.com/affiliate)).

**Kalshi has effectively closed its door to small affiliates.** Its commission is negotiated individually with no public rate, and it pulled affiliate badges from X in February 2026 after X tightened its rules on gambling promotion ([CasinoBeats](https://casinobeats.com/2026/02/24/kalshi-pulls-affiliate-badges-as-x-tightens-gambling-promotions-rules/)). For you, Kalshi is an RAF play, not an affiliate one. Sports event contracts are restricted in some states (reported as MI, NV and WA), and a Nevada court ordered Kalshi to halt operations there in March 2026 ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/prediction/news/prediction-market-legal-states/); [Fox5 Vegas](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/03/23/court-orders-prediction-market-app-kalshi-halt-operations-nevada)).

**DFS sits in the middle.** PrizePicks' partner program "suits creators with engaged audiences in sports, betting, or gaming," with rates "negotiable based on audience size." A qualifying first-time depositor reportedly registers and deposits $10+ ([UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/prizepicks/)). PrizePicks is hiring entry-level staff to source creators, so it is actively recruiting ([BuiltIn](https://builtin.com/job/strategic-partnerships-contractor-entry-level/7436284)). ParlayPlay's public self-registration form makes it the easiest DFS application. Its terms are set by an account manager, the most recent review is stale (spring 2025), and the app is unavailable in HI, ID, LA, MT, NV, OR and WA ([gaffg](https://gaffg.com/affiliate-program/parlayplay-affiliates/); [Covers](https://www.covers.com/betting/bonuses/parlayplay-promo-code)). Underdog clearly runs an affiliate program (it is hiring an Affiliate Manager), but no public signup page was found ([SV Angel jobs](https://jobs.svangel.com/companies/underdog-fantasy/jobs/44806436-affiliate-manager)).

**Discord itself is the weak spot in every gambling application.** Programs judge private-channel traffic as "concentrated, opaque, and harder to verify than a content site," and they require pre-approved creative, no guaranteed-win claims and responsible-gambling notices ([track360 Telegram guide](https://track360.io/blog/sports-betting-tipster-telegram-affiliate-channel-operator-guide-2026)). Some networks require a website, and a simple landing page helps ([RichAds](https://richads.com/blog/how-to-become-a-sports-betting-affiliate-2026/)). No US program was found that explicitly allows or bans Discord.

### Fintech, brokerage and cash-back

Getting into a network is easy. Getting approved by each brand inside it is the real filter. **FlexOffers** judges applicants without a website on "the volume and detail of the answers you provided," requires every traffic source to be live and reviewable, approves each social page separately for each advertiser, and usually decides in 1–2 business days ([FlexOffers support](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/is-my-traffic-source-suitable-for-flexoffers)). It pays **Net 60**, so March commissions arrive in early June ([FlexOffers payment](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/payment)). **Impact** is free to join with no network-level minimums, but "some programs require a minimum traffic threshold or a live website," and its payout minimum is reportedly about $10 ([AffNinja](https://affninja.com/impact-affiliate-review/)). **CJ** reportedly won't accept a social profile as your main promotional method, so a custom-domain site is the reliable route ([affililist](https://www.affililist.com/blog/cj-affiliate-program)). **Webull** requires an existing Webull account to apply and targets "content creators or media publishers" ([Webull](https://act.webullapp.com/mktb/partners/us/individual)). **Acorns** offers a 30-day cookie and an account manager but publishes no CPA ([Acorns](https://acorns.com/affiliate-program)). **Credit cards are off the table**: Amex wants a valid website, and Discover reportedly requires a finance-focused site with US traffic ([UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/blog/credit-card-affiliate-programs)). Cash-back apps pay little through affiliate channels (Ibotta about $2–$5) ([UpPromote Ibotta](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/ibotta)), which is another reason to use Rakuten's $50 RAF instead.

## Licensing, disclosure and 21+ rules apply to a group chat too

**Choose CPA, never revenue share, if you ever join a sportsbook program.** In most regulated states, how you're paid determines how hard you are to license. In New Jersey, flat-fee affiliates can hold a Vendor Registration, while revenue-share affiliates need an Ancillary CSIE license ([Ifrah Law](https://www.ifrahlaw.com/ifrah-on-igaming/new-jersey-dge-releases-guidance-for-affiliate-marketers/)). Reported CPA-tier costs are about **$200 in Michigan, $350 in Colorado (Vendor Minor) and $500 in Virginia**. Revenue-share tiers run into the thousands, plus background checks ([BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/affiliate/); [Szilaghi](https://szilaghi.com/media-affiliate-gaming-license-cost-in-the-us/)). **Pennsylvania is expensive even for CPA**: the PGCB snippet shows $2,000 plus a $500 entity fee plus $60 per qualifier just to register ([PGCB](https://gamingcontrolboard.pa.gov/licensing/gaming-related-service-providers-information)). Some guides list NY (CPA only), OH and IL as needing no affiliate license, and report that Indiana stopped enforcing affiliate registration in July 2025. Sources conflict on all of these ([Vantegris](https://vantegris.com/blog/us-affiliate-licence-state-by-state/); [SBC Americas](https://sbcamericas.com/2025/06/02/indiana-scraps-some-betting-licenses/)). **No source found a small-affiliate exemption, so assume none.** DFS affiliate registration is lighter: one 2026 source names only NJ, NY and MA, and another says DFS affiliates generally need no registration ([track360 DFS](https://track360.io/blog/daily-fantasy-sports-operator-affiliate-playbook-2026)). The research did not report any vendor registration requirement for consumer RAF programs, which is a further reason to start there.

**Disclose every link, every time, in the message itself.** The FTC's 2023 Endorsement Guides require disclosure of any material connection, commissions and referral bonuses included, in a way that is "difficult to miss" and "easily understandable." **"#ad" and "Paid link" next to the link are acceptable. "Affiliate link" alone is not** ([Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/ftc-updates-faqs-for-endorsement-guides-offers-more-guidance-on-social-media-and-video-endorsements); [Manatt](https://manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/an-in-depth-look-at-the-ftcs-updates-2)). The rule doesn't depend on platform or audience size, so it covers a text to a friend just as it covers a Discord post. A pinned channel disclosure is a supplement, not a substitute. For banks and brokerages, use the brand's own wording for bonus terms rather than paraphrasing, since regulators judge the ad together with the signup flow ([Sedric](https://www.sedric.ai/blog/ensuring-compliance-in-affiliate-and-partner-marketing-for-financial-services-a-strategic-guide)).

**Responsible-gaming copy is mandatory for anything betting-related.** The American Gaming Association's code bans "risk-free" language, bars college partnerships, and requires anyone featured in ads to be 21+ ([Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/109587/aga-tightens-up-sports-betting-ad-responsible-marketing-code/)). Its September 2025 version allows social and web marketing only where **at least 73.6% of the audience is reasonably expected to be 21+**, and AGA members must pass the code on to their affiliates ([AGA Code PDF](https://www.americangaming.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Responsible-Gaming-Code-of-Conduct-2026.pdf)). Colorado requires "Gambling problem? Call or TEXT 1-800-GAMBLER" on all advertising, including affiliates' ([Colorado SB Rule 9](https://sbg.colorado.gov/sites/sbg/files/documents/SBRule-9-011426.pdf)). Massachusetts bars calling a promotion "free" when users risk their own money, and it fined Penn $25,000 over a "Can't Lose Parlay" ad ([Regulatory Review](https://www.theregreview.org/2025/09/06/seminar-how-should-regulators-address-sports-betting-advertisements/)). For a picks channel, that means **no "lock," "guaranteed," "can't lose" or "risk-free,"** and you should gate gambling channels to verified 21+ members. If some of your 38 friends are under 21 or in college, keep them out of the betting side entirely.

**Taxes start with the first dollar.** All affiliate and referral income is taxable whether or not you get a 1099. For payments made in 2026, the 1099-NEC/MISC issuance threshold rose from $600 to **$2,000** under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act ([Patriot Software](https://www.patriotsoftware.com/blog/accounting/1099-reporting-threshold/)). Affiliate programs will ask for a W-9. Based on general tax knowledge not sourced in this research, a free IRS EIN lets you avoid handing out your SSN, commissions usually go on Schedule C, and self-employment tax (about 15.3%) applies once net earnings reach $400. An LLC isn't required. It mainly helps with state vendor forms built for entities, and California's $800 minimum franchise tax shows why it isn't worth forming one for a 38-person audience.

## Four hard no's that trigger clawbacks or criminal exposure

**Never refer yourself, and never touch a friend's account, device or payment method.** A "player flagged as a self-referral or linked to the affiliate account" is a standard clawback trigger, and CPA clawback windows typically run 30–90 days ([track360 deal structures](https://track360.io/blog/igaming-affiliate-deal-structures-operator-guide-2026)). FanDuel's consumer referral terms bar referring immediate family or household members ([FanDuel](https://www.fanduel.com/sportsbook-referral-2024)), and Robinhood can rescind rewards over suspected fraud ([Robinhood support](https://robinhood.com/support/articles/invite-friends-get-free-stock)). Thirty-eight friends signing up in one week from the same Wi-Fi, after a game-night "sign-up party," is exactly what fraud teams look for. Each friend should sign up on their own phone, with their own money, and actually use the product.

**Never pay friends to sign up or split your commission with them.** Offers labeled "Non-Incent," such as FlexOffers' Stash listing, explicitly ban rewarding users for signing up ([FlexOffers Stash](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/stash-invest-iphone-us-non-incent-affiliate-program/)). Standard affiliate terms commonly ban incentivized referrals and messaging-app blasts ([Kommunicate terms](https://www.kommunicate.io/partners/affiliate/terms/)). "Sign up with my link and I'll Venmo you $20" turns legitimate traffic into a terms violation. The RAF bonus the friend gets from the app itself is the only sweetener you should offer.

**Don't promote sweepstakes casinos at all.** Track360 rates Stake.us, McLuck and Pulsz at $35–$110 CPA ([track360 rate card](https://track360.io/blog/sweepstakes-affiliate-program-rate-card-benchmark-2026)), but the 2025–2026 bans target promoters directly. **California AB 831** (effective Jan 1, 2026) makes it a misdemeanor for media affiliates to knowingly promote sweepstakes casinos, punishable by up to a year in jail and **$25,000 per violation** ([ZwillGen](https://www.zwillgen.com/gaming/californias-ab-831-bans-sweepstakes-casinos-expands-liability-vendors/)). **Louisiana** (Aug 1, 2026) sets up to $20,000 and five years for promoters, **Oklahoma** (Nov 1, 2026) makes it a felony, and **New York, Maine and Indiana** impose civil penalties of $10,000–$100,000 per violation ([CasinoReports](https://www.casinoreports.com/news/sweepstakes-ban-2026-state-roundup/); [InfoLawGroup](https://www.infolawgroup.com/insights/2026/7/27/sweepstakes-casino-laws-in-2026-new-bans-proposed-legislation-and-regulatory-trends)). A Discord can't geo-restrict its members, so one California member could turn a $50 CPA into a criminal matter.

**Don't post picks as promises, and don't treat X as safe ground.** Guaranteed-win claims violate operator creative rules and state ad codes. X tightened its rules on gambling-affiliate promotion in February 2026, which is why Kalshi's affiliates lost their badges ([CasinoBeats](https://casinobeats.com/2026/02/24/kalshi-pulls-affiliate-badges-as-x-tightens-gambling-promotions-rules/)). Check the platform's rules before you build a public picks account.

That timing gap is one more reason to lead with RAF.

## Conclusion

The core insight is that **at 38 people, your edge is trust, not traffic, and the industry pays for trust through referral programs, not affiliate contracts.** Affiliate programs are built to buy anonymous traffic at scale. They price a tiny, verifiable friend network poorly, and they apply exactly the fraud filters that a burst of signups from one friend group trips. The sensible order is to collect the RAF money first, then use that period to build the public assets (a site, a picks record, a 21+ gated community) that affiliate managers actually review. That's what turns a group chat into something PrizePicks or a sportsbook would sign.

The second takeaway is about direction. Gambling-adjacent affiliate work is getting harder: sweepstakes bans now name promoters, X has restricted gambling promotion, Kalshi has pulled back from small affiliates, and prediction markets face state-by-state litigation. Fintech and cash-back referrals carry none of that legal exposure and currently pay as much per head.
