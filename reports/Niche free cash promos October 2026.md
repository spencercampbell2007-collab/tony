## Fine print that turns "free" into a loss

The most common way to lose value is **expiry**. Kalshi referral credits die in 7 days, Polymarket credit in 7–30 days, Sleeper's match in 30 days, and Pick6-style bonus picks in 14. The second is **redemption floors**: Fliff Cash and most sweeps coins need $50–100 accumulated before you can cash out, so a small free balance often can't be withdrawn at all. The third is **ID checks**. Every withdrawal path, whether prediction market, DFS or sweeps, requires ID and address verification, so verify before you build a balance, while your state still allows play. Watch for **mismatched codes**: the same app shows different offers depending on which affiliate link you use, so pick the best one and confirm it in-app before you deposit. Avoid **offshore books** that show up in "new betting sites" lists (Lucky Rebel, BetNow, Busr), which are not US-licensed ([football-espana.net](https://www.football-espana.net/betting-sites/new-sports/)). Treat "**gift card generator**" pages as malware traps. One was found hosted on a hijacked mit.edu wiki ([glycan.mit.edu](https://glycan.mit.edu/CFGparadigms/index.php/FREE_Uber_Eats_Gift_Card_Generator_PRO_APK_(2025))). **Freecash** was pulled from both app stores after TechCrunch scrutiny, and its privacy policy permits collecting sensitive data ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/how-the-rewards-app-freecash-scammed-its-way-to-the-top-of-the-app-stores/); [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/01/get-paid-to-scroll-tiktok-the-data-trade-behind-freecash-ads)). The sweeps sportsbooks carry real counterparty risk: Fliff has received cease-and-desists from Arizona and reportedly New York, and faces a California class action (sent to arbitration) ([Sweepsy](https://www.sweepsy.com/?p=13555); [LSR](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/119758/lawsuit-fliff-sued-over-illegal-sports-betting-in-california/)). Keep deposits there to the $5–10 needed to unlock the bonus.

## Conclusion

For someone who already holds every mainstream sportsbook, the opportunity has shifted from the sportsbooks to the **prediction-market products those same brands launched in 2026**. These products are in a customer-acquisition phase, which is why a $5 trade can unlock $100–200 in credits while the matching sportsbook offers are already used up. That window will not last. Offers have already been cut (Polymarket went from $50 to $25 on Oct 1, and DK Pick6's offer ended Oct 18), and the legal fight between states and the CFTC can geofence a product overnight. Claim the $5-unlock prediction and DFS offers first, while they cost almost nothing to try.

Treat sweeps coins and AI-launch stunts as occasional extras rather than reliable income. Sweeps value is shrinking with each new state ban. Launch promos like Hark's last about a day and ask for a lot of personal data in exchange for about $30. Before you act on any line here, **open the official in-app offer page and confirm the amount, the expiry and that your state is eligible**. Every figure in this report passed through an affiliate site, and none was confirmed on an official terms page.
