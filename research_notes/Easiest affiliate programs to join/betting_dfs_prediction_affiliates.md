## 1. Which operators run open (self-serve) affiliate programs, and how do you apply?

### Takeaway
None of the big sportsbooks or pick'em operators has a truly open, auto-approve program. They all review applications by hand, mostly on Income Access panels (FanDuel, Hard Rock, Caesars, BetRivers) or their own portals (bet365, ParlayPlay). PrizePicks and Underdog use direct partner/creator deals. The closest thing to an open program for a beginner is **Polymarket's Dub-powered affiliate program** (rolling applications; guides describe a low bar). ParlayPlay also has a public self-registration form.

### Cited Findings
**Prediction markets**
- **Polymarket:** launched an official affiliate program "powered by Dub" in late Nov 2025. Applications go to partners.dub.co/polymarket, with "a second batch... accepted on rolling & case by case basis" — [Steven Tey (Dub CEO) on X](https://x.com/steventey/status/1992678397466804554). He also says there are thousands of affiliates on X with Polymarket badges — [X post](https://x.com/steventey/status/1989810048777031862).
  - Payout terms CONFLICT. Community/third-party guides say **$10 per referral who deposits $20, plus $0.01 per click** — [Nira on X](https://x.com/NiraGustira/status/1999516058995294438); [polymarket-partners.com FAQ (community-partner site, not official)](https://polymarket-partners.com/faq). Another 2026 guide says **10% of net trading fees from direct referrals and 5% from indirect ones, paid daily in pUSD, with a $10,000 lifetime-volume requirement to join** — [polymira.org](https://polymira.org/earn/polymarket-affiliate). Gamblingaffiliatehub also cites the 10%/5% split — [gamblingaffiliatehub prediction markets](https://gamblingaffiliatehub.com/vertical/prediction-markets/).

**Sweepstakes casinos**

A track360 benchmark estimates CPA of about $50–$110 and revshare of about 25–35% net of redemptions — [track360 rate card](https://track360.io/blog/sweepstakes-affiliate-program-rate-card-benchmark-2026).
- **McLuck:** estimated CPA of about $35–$90 (another source says $30–$50 per FTD) and revshare of about 25–30%. - **Pulsz:** track360 estimates CPA of about $40–$90 and revshare of about 25–35%, contradicted by AskGamblers, which lists CPA and hybrid as "No" — [AskGamblers Pulsz Affiliates](https://www.askgamblers.com/casino-affiliate-programs/pulsz-affiliates-review).

### Inferences
- Ease-of-approval ranking for a beginner with about 38 contacts and a Discord (my judgment from the evidence above):
  1. **Polymarket (Dub)**: rolling applications, creator/X-native, small bounties. Easiest, *if* the $10k-volume requirement claim is false or met, and if Polymarket is legal in the user's state (not verified here).
  2. **Sweepstakes casinos (McLuck, Stake.us, Pulsz)**: offshore-style affiliate programs historically accept social/Telegram traffic and offer CPA or revshare without heavy state licensing. Rates are unverified, and sweeps legality is shrinking state by state (not researched here).
  3. **ParlayPlay**: public self-signup form, but terms are negotiated and the evidence is stale.
  4. **PrizePicks / Underdog creator programs**: manual approval aimed at creators with "engaged audiences". A 38-person chat likely falls below the bar, but they actively recruit, so pitching a niche Discord is plausible.
  5. **Kalshi**: negotiated deals, and it de-emphasized X affiliates in 2026.
  6. **Big books (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, bet365, BetRivers, Hard Rock, Fanatics)**: hardest. Manual compliance review, state vendor registration, and they favor content sites and media. Likely rejection without a website.
- For 38 friends, each operator's **consumer refer-a-friend** program (e.g., Fanatics $100/referral capped at 5; Dabble $30) may pay more, more reliably, than a beginner affiliate deal. That is out of scope, but it is the honest alternative.

### Gaps
- No official, page-level terms were readable for any program (WebFetch blocked). Underdog, Sleeper, Dabble, Betr, Boom, Chalkboard, Fanatics, Novig and Fliff affiliate application URLs and terms were not found.
- Polymarket's current US availability and per-state restrictions were not verified. Its payout model ($10 bounty vs. 10% fee share) is unresolved.

## 2. Which accept tiny/new affiliates or run creator/ambassador programs, and which reject without a site or traffic?

### Takeaway
Creator-style programs (Polymarket/Dub, PrizePicks and Underdog partnerships, Kalshi sponsorships) are built for social creators rather than websites. Regulated sportsbooks review manually, require compliance pre-approval and state registration, and some programs want a website.

### Cited Findings
- PrizePicks' program "suits creators with engaged audiences in sports, betting, or gaming", such as livestreamers and fantasy experts, with rates "negotiable based on audience size" — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/prizepicks/).
- Polymarket affiliates are largely X-based accounts with "Polymarket badges"; approval is "rolling & case by case" — [X/Steven Tey](https://x.com/steventey/status/1992678397466804554).
- Telegram and Discord: "most sportsbook affiliate programs accept traffic from social media, streaming platforms, Telegram channels" (offshore/international programs such as 22Bet and PIN-UP list Telegram) — [UpPromote sports betting list](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-programs/sports-betting/); [RichAds](https://richads.com/blog/sports-betting-affiliate-programs/). However, "some affiliate networks and programs do require a website for approval", and a simple landing page can help — [RichAds beginner guide](https://richads.com/blog/how-to-become-a-sports-betting-affiliate-2026/). Some programs require written consent for each social platform — [bitcoincasinoaffiliates](https://bitcoincasinoaffiliates.com/).
- A tipster/Telegram guide notes operators typically require pre-approved creative, no guaranteed-win claims, and responsible-gambling notices. It adds that channel traffic is "concentrated, opaque, and harder to verify than a content site" — [track360 Telegram tipster guide](https://track360.io/blog/sports-betting-tipster-telegram-affiliate-channel-operator-guide-2026).
- US books: applications are reviewed manually, creatives need state compliance approval, and traffic is geo-gated so out-of-state clicks don't pay — [track360 DraftKings](https://track360.io/blog/draftkings-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026). FanDuel CPA and hybrid deals "must pass compliance checks and meet traffic quality standards" — [track360 FanDuel](https://track360.io/blog/fanduel-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026).
- **State licensing (major beginner barrier):** in NJ, a CPA (flat-fee) affiliate needs a free Vendor Registration that the operator files for it. A revshare or hybrid affiliate needs an Ancillary CSIE license with a $2,000 filing fee — [Vantegris state guide](https://vantegris.com/blog/us-affiliate-licence-state-by-state/); [Ifrah Law](https://www.ifrahlaw.com/ifrah-on-igaming/needs-license-online-sports-betting-gaming-new-jersey/); [NJ AG sports wagering FAQ (primary)](https://www.nj.gov/oag/ge/docs/SportsBetting/FAQs.pdf). One guide says OH, NC, NY, IA, WY, NV and DC require nothing; BettingUSA also lists Ohio as "No License Necessary". This is conflicting and unverified with regulators — [BettingUSA affiliate guide](https://www.bettingusa.com/affiliate/).
- X restricted gambling-affiliate promotion in Feb 2026, causing Kalshi affiliates to lose badges — [CasinoBeats](https://casinobeats.com/2026/02/24/kalshi-pulls-affiliate-badges-as-x-tightens-gambling-promotions-rules/).

### Inferences
- A private Discord of about 38 friends is "closed/opaque traffic". Regulated sportsbook programs will likely want a public presence (a simple site or public social account) to review compliance. A basic landing page plus public X/IG/TikTok picks account would improve approval odds everywhere.
- Prediction markets and DFS are not state-licensed sportsbooks, so state vendor registration likely doesn't apply in the same way. This was not verified.

### Gaps
- No program was found to explicitly state "Discord allowed" or "Discord banned" for US regulated books. No published minimum traffic numbers were found for any program.

## 3. Typical 2026 CPA ranges, and who pays CPA on small volume?

### Takeaway
Reported 2026 CPAs are about $100–$500 per FTD for major regulated sportsbooks (state-dependent and negotiated), about $25–$100 for DFS/pick'em, about $30–$110 for sweepstakes casinos, and about $10 bounties or a fee share for Polymarket. New affiliates typically start on CPA. All figures come from third-party sources and conflict.

### Cited Findings
- DraftKings: one directory says a flat $250 CPA, $100 minimum payout and 45-day cookie — [UpPromote DraftKings](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/draftkings/). A 2026 review instead reports a $100–$500 band by state, vertical and tier, and revshare of 20–35% of NGR — [track360 DraftKings](https://track360.io/blog/draftkings-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026). (These conflict.)
- FanDuel: "up to 35% RevShare or $25–35 CPA" ([15m.com](https://15m.com/affiliate-programs/fanduel-affiliate/)) vs. $100–$500 per FTD ([track360 FanDuel](https://track360.io/blog/fanduel-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026)). Reported cookie windows run 30–730 days.
- BetMGM: about $100–$400 per qualifying FTD, negotiated and unpublished. New affiliates typically start CPA-only for 30–90 days before being offered a hybrid — [track360 BetMGM](https://track360.io/blog/betmgm-affiliate-program-operator-review-2026).
- Typical payment: monthly, roughly Net-30 to Net-45, with minimum payouts of about $50–$100 — [search summary of StatsDrone/track360](https://statsdrone.com/best-affiliate-programs/sports-betting/).
- DFS: $25–$100 per FTD with a qualifying entry; hybrids and revshare on rake also exist — [track360 DFS playbook](https://track360.io/blog/daily-fantasy-sports-operator-affiliate-playbook-2026). PrizePicks: $25–$60+ — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/prizepicks/).
- Sweeps: Stake.us about $50–$110 CPA; McLuck about $35–$90; Pulsz about $40–$90; revshare about 25–35% net of redemptions — [track360 rate card](https://track360.io/blog/sweepstakes-affiliate-program-rate-card-benchmark-2026).
- Polymarket: $10 per $20 depositor plus $0.01 per click, or 10%/5% of fees (conflicting) — see Section 1.

### Inferences
- At 38 referrals, an optimistic CPA case is roughly 38 × $25–$60 ≈ $950–$2,300 at DFS. Realistically, only part of the 38 will deposit, and the same people can't be counted at multiple operators unless they sign up for each. Big-book CPAs are higher, but approval is unlikely at this size.
- The programs most likely to pay CPA on very small volume are Polymarket (flat bounty, daily payout per guides) and sweeps programs. Regulated books typically negotiate CPA only for vetted partners.

### Gaps
- No verified minimum-FTD counts were found for CPA eligibility at any US program. Payment thresholds for Polymarket, Kalshi and the sweeps programs were not found.

## 4. Red flags: clawbacks, minimum thresholds, bans on incentivized or self-referral/friends-and-family traffic

### Takeaway
Self-referral and linked-account players are a standard clawback trigger, and friends sharing devices or households with the affiliate can be flagged. Negative carryover and monthly active-player minimums (e.g., bet365) hurt small affiliates on revshare. Taking CPA rather than revshare avoids carryover.

### Cited Findings
- Clawback triggers include a "player flagged as a self-referral or linked to the affiliate account". CPA clawback windows are typically 30–90 days — [track360 deal structures guide](https://track360.io/blog/igaming-affiliate-deal-structures-operator-guide-2026).
- bet365 Partners reportedly applies negative carryover and requires **at least 15 active depositing players a month** to keep getting paid — [track360 bet365](https://track360.io/blog/bet365-affiliate-program-us-operator-analysis-2026). Programs with no negative carryover "are few and far between" — [Affiliate Bible](https://www.affiliatebible.com/sportsbook/sportsbook-affiliate-programs-with-no-negative-carryover.php); [aff.studio](https://aff.studio/directory/betting-affiliate-programs/no-negative-carryover).
- FanDuel's *consumer* referral terms bar referring immediate family or household members — [FanDuel referral terms](https://www.fanduel.com/sportsbook-referral-2024). DraftKings' consumer referral reportedly has no family restriction — [bettingtools](https://bettingtools.com/us/guides/refer-a-friend/). These are RAF programs, and the affiliate T&Cs may differ.
- DFS operators flag bonus abuse on free-contest signups as a key risk — [track360 DFS playbook](https://track360.io/blog/daily-fantasy-sports-operator-affiliate-playbook-2026).
- Polymarket community "join" sites asking for deposits through their own referral links should be treated with caution (search summary). Several pages are run by "community partners", not Polymarket — [polymarket-partners.com](https://polymarket-partners.com/).
- Pick'em entries still face state limits: ParlayPlay is unavailable in 7 states ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/betting/bonuses/parlayplay-promo-code)), and some Underdog offers exclude MD, MI, NJ, NY, OH and PA ([Underdog help center](https://help.underdogsports.com/en/articles/8974179-complete-guide-to-underdog-promotions-bonuses-and-reward-terms)).

### Inferences
- The user's core plan (about 38 real friends signing up through their link) is exactly the pattern fraud teams scrutinize: same IPs or devices, shared payment methods, and low-value one-time deposits. Friends must use their own devices and payment methods and play genuinely, or CPA may be clawed back.
- Running a CPA-only deal avoids negative carryover. Posting "picks" in a group chat needs to avoid guaranteed-win language and include responsible-gambling messaging to stay compliant.
- Paying friends (e.g., kicking back part of the CPA) would be incentivized traffic and is commonly banned. No specific program clause on this was found.

### Gaps
- No actual affiliate T&C text (as opposed to RAF terms) was read for any US operator. Explicit "friends and family" and "incentivized traffic" clauses are unconfirmed.
- Restricted-state lists for each affiliate program were not compiled beyond the examples above.
