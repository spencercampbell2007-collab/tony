## Which apps pay the most per signup via affiliate programs?

### Takeaway
The commonly listed per-signup affiliate payouts are modest: about $16–$20 per funded account for Robinhood, about $40 per account deposit for Moomoo (FlexOffers), "up to" $75–$100 claimed for Chime/SoFi depending on action, $2–$5 for Ibotta/Fetch, and revenue share rather than flat CPA for crypto (Kraken 20% lifetime, Coinbase 50% of fees for 3 months). In many cases **the consumer refer-a-friend bonus pays the same as or more than the affiliate CPA** (e.g., Rakuten RAF $50, Chime RAF $100), and it needs no approval.

## Which networks accept brand-new affiliates with little/no traffic, and what's needed to get approved?

### Takeaway
Joining a network is relatively easy. FlexOffers and Impact have no published traffic minimums, but **each advertiser approves you separately**, and finance advertisers are the strictest. A simple live website on a custom domain, with real finance or deals content, an about page, a privacy policy, and affiliate disclosures, is the safest path. Social-only applicants are judged case by case, and CJ reportedly discourages them.

### Cited Findings
- **FlexOffers:**
  - Applicants who do not promote through a website are judged on "other similar criteria… based on the volume and detail of the answers you provided in your application."
  - Traffic sources must be live and reviewable ("Our team must be able to access and review your traffic source").
  - Each traffic source, such as social media (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest), must be approved per advertiser.
  - Approval usually takes 1–2 business days, per FlexOffers. — [FlexOffers support: suitable traffic source](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/is-my-traffic-source-suitable-for-flexoffers); [FlexOffers: becoming a publisher](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/becoming-a-publisher); [FlexOffers: why application declined](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/why-was-my-publisher-application-declined)
  - A third party says there are "no minimum traffic or sales requirements" (unverified). — [Authority Hacker](https://authorityhacker.com/flexoffers-affiliate/)
- **FlexOffers payment:** **Net 60** monthly (official); for example, March commissions are paid in the first week of June. Net 30 or Net 7 is possible for top performers (official). The threshold is not stated officially; third parties say **$25** for PayPal/direct deposit (one says $50). — [FlexOffers support: payment](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/payment); [FlexOffers compensation](https://www.flexoffers.com/publishers/compensation); [UpPromote FlexOffers](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/flexoffers/)
- **Impact.com:** free to join, with no disclosed network-level traffic or sales minimums. "You'll also need to meet each brand's individual approval criteria. Some programs require a minimum traffic threshold or a live website with relevant content." The payment minimum is reportedly about $10. — [AffNinja Impact review 2026](https://affninja.com/impact-affiliate-review/); [UpPromote Chime (Impact $10 threshold)](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-programs/chime/)
- **CJ Affiliate:** social and email traffic are allowed if registered in your profile. However, affililist (2026) says you "cannot apply to CJ with a social media profile alone as your primary promotional method" and that a self-hosted site on a custom domain is the most reliable route. Network approval "does not give you the right to promote any specific product." The payout threshold is reportedly about $50. — [affililist CJ approval tips 2026](https://www.affililist.com/blog/cj-affiliate-program); [PW Skills CJ guide](https://pwskills.com/blog/digital-marketing/commission-junction-cj)
- **Direct programs:** Webull requires an existing Webull account plus a creator application (official). Rakuten Creator Collective requires 1,500+ followers on one platform (official). Kraken is reportedly open to anyone. — sources above.

### Inferences
- Likely order of ease: Kraken (open) > FlexOffers (beginner-friendly, accepts non-website sources based on application detail) > Impact (easy network join, but brand approval required) > CJ (prefers a real website) > credit card issuers (need an established finance site).
- A Discord server alone is not a listed source type for FlexOffers (its list is website and social media). Discord usually has to be described as an "other" traffic source and is likely to be rejected by finance advertisers. A basic deals or finance website with a privacy policy and disclosures, plus the Discord as a secondary source, is a stronger application.

### Gaps
- No official Impact or CJ policy pages were retrieved. Impact's $10 and CJ's $50 thresholds come from third-party snippets.
- No source confirmed whether Discord is explicitly an acceptable traffic type on any network.

## Which programs prohibit friends-and-family, incentivized traffic, coupon/deal sites, or Discord promotion?

### Takeaway
Explicit fintech-specific terms were mostly not retrievable. Common patterns are: no self-referrals and no household or controlled accounts; "non-incent" finance offers, which ban rewarding users for signing up (e.g., Stash "Non-Incent"); and bans on spam or messaging-app blasts. FlexOffers' Robinhood and Moomoo listings say "restrictions apply," and the details were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- FlexOffers' Stash program is explicitly titled **"Stash Invest iPhone US Non-Incent,"** which means incentivized traffic is prohibited. — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/stash-invest-iphone-us-non-incent-affiliate-program/)
- The FlexOffers Robinhood ($16) and Moomoo ($40) listings note "restrictions" (content not retrieved). — [FlexOffers Robinhood](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/robinhood-affiliate-program/); [FlexOffers Moomoo](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/moomoo-affiliate-program/)
- Webull referral terms exclude Webull employees, related entities, and their households, and allow Webull to end the program anytime without notice. — [Webull partners (official)](https://act.webullapp.com/mktb/partners/us/individual) (snippet)
- FlexOffers requires each traffic source, including each social page, to be approved for each advertiser before you promote there. — [FlexOffers support](https://supportbeta.flexoffers.com/knowledge/is-my-traffic-source-suitable-for-flexoffers)
- General affiliate terms (non-fintech examples) commonly ban self-referrals, purchases by your household or accounts you control, incentivized clicks or referrals, and "messaging-app blasts" or social DMs. — [verify.trading terms](https://verify-trading.getrewardful.com/terms); [Freaking Nomads](https://freakingnomads.com/affiliate-program-terms); [PricePush terms](https://pricepush.app/affiliate-terms); [Kommunicate terms](https://www.kommunicate.io/partners/affiliate/terms/)
- Credit card issuers (Discover via CJ) reportedly require a finance-focused site with US traffic, which effectively excludes Discord-only or coupon-style publishers. — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/blog/credit-card-affiliate-programs)

### Inferences
- Sharing an affiliate link with real friends is generally not banned by these terms. What is typically banned is self-signup, household or controlled accounts, and paying or rewarding people to sign up (e.g., "I'll split my commission with you" would be incentivized traffic). The user should read each program's terms in the Impact, FlexOffers, or CJ dashboard before posting in a Discord.
- Bank and brokerage brands commonly restrict coupon or deal sites and "paid search on brand terms." This could not be confirmed here.

### Gaps
- No program-specific terms for Robinhood, Chime, SoFi, Acorns, Coinbase, or Moomoo on Discord, coupon sites, or friends and family could be retrieved (WebFetch blocked).

Affiliate CPA wins mainly for Robinhood ($16–$20 vs. about $10 historical RAF) and possibly Moomoo ($40 CPA), and it is the only option for apps without a strong RAF.
