**Tax (brief):** Bank bonuses are generally reported as interest on a 1099-INT. Brokerage stock/cash bonuses usually go on a 1099-MISC (or 1099-B when the stock is sold). Cash-back apps typically issue a 1099 only above reporting thresholds, and purchase rebates are often treated as non-taxable price reductions. Only the general point that "bonuses are generally taxable income" was confirmed in this search ([Yahoo/roundup summary via search](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/banking/article/new-bank-account-bonuses-214704427.html)). The form-type details above are general knowledge and were not sourced in this session.

## Q5. Red flags: payout complaints and data harvesting

### Takeaway
Freecash is the clearest red flag. Fetch openly monetizes purchase data. No issues were found for Upside or Mistplay.

### Cited Findings
- **Freecash data collection:** the privacy policy permits collection of sensitive data (race, religion, sexual orientation, health, biometrics). The parent company is Almedia (Berlin). — [Malwarebytes Jan 2026](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2026/01/get-paid-to-scroll-tiktok-the-data-trade-behind-freecash-ads)
- **Freecash app-store removal:** removed from the App Store and Google Play after TechCrunch scrutiny. Almedia disputes wrongdoing and blames affiliates for misleading TikTok ads. — [TechCrunch 2026-04-14](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/how-the-rewards-app-freecash-scammed-its-way-to-the-top-of-the-app-stores/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://pocketgamer.biz/freecash-removed-from-app-store-and-google-play-after-data-scrutiny/)
- **Freecash complaints:** offer-tracking failures, account bans, and a BBB "pattern of complaints," despite a high Trustpilot score. — [Finder Freecash review](https://www.finder.com/make-money-online/freecash-review)
- **Fetch:** a watchdog site says selling consumer data to brands is Fetch's business model (anonymized and aggregated). No 2026 enforcement was found. — [terms.law](https://terms.law/Privacy-Watchdog/cashback-apps/fetch/); [CompassITC](https://compassitc.com/blog/is-the-fetch-rewards-app-safe-exploring-data-privacy-concerns)
- **Robinhood:** the referral terms allow rescinding rewards for suspected fraud. — [Robinhood support](https://robinhood.com/support/articles/invite-friends-get-free-stock)
- **Bank push-DD:** banks change their ACH filters without notice. — [TheBonusStack](https://thebonusstack.com/learn/what-counts-as-direct-deposit-bank-bonuses)

### Inferences
- Avoid Freecash or use it with a throwaway profile.
- Stash and Acorns charge monthly fees that can eat a $20–$25 bonus if the account isn't closed.

### Gaps
- No data was found on Upside or Mistplay payout disputes or data practices.
