## Red flags: redemption complaints, legal crackdowns, KYC traps

### Takeaway
The main risks are state exits that freeze accounts into redeem-only mode, ongoing litigation (Stake.us), and unsettled state-versus-CFTC fights over sports event contracts. Those fights can geofence prediction apps (MD, NV, MA) mid-promo. Prediction-market bonuses are non-withdrawable credits that require KYC before any profit can be withdrawn.

- The Illinois Gaming Board sent cease-and-desist letters to 65 sweeps operators on Feb 5, 2026. Stake.us left IL on May 19, 2026, and accounts went **redeem-only**: no play, purchases, promos or free-SC codes. It faces player suits tied to Drake and Adin Ross (MO, NM, VA, NJ), an LA City Attorney action, and an Ohio federal suit filed in early 2026 — [iGaming Future](https://igamingfuture.com/sweepstakes-casinos/news/i-tracked-every-stake-us-lawsuit-and-state-exit-heres-what-players-need-to-do/)

### Inferences
- Short credit expiry windows (Kalshi referral credits at 7 days, Polymarket at 7 to 30 days) are the most common way bonus value is lost.
