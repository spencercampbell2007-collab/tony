# Niche / Regional US Sportsbook & DFS Sign-up Bonuses (as of Oct 19, 2026)

**Method note:** Every WebFetch attempt failed with DNS errors (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for cbssports.com and gambling.com). **All facts below come only from WebSearch result snippets and summaries.** Nearly all sources are affiliate promo pages, so the codes are affiliate tracking codes and the terms often conflict. Treat every offer as "verify in app before depositing." EV figures are my own estimates (Inferences), not sourced numbers.

EV assumptions used in the Inferences sections:
- A non-withdrawable sportsbook bonus bet converts at ~70% (the user-supplied rule of thumb).
- A DFS/pick'em or sweeps deposit match with 1x playthrough is worth roughly 75–90% of face value, because you only lose the house edge on one cycle. Pick'em hold varies by entry type, so I use ~80%.
- A "bonus entry/bonus pick" that pays out only winnings (stake not returned) is worth ~50–70% of face, depending on the payout structure.

## 4. Red flags

### Takeaway
Fliff, Thrillzz and Rebet are sweepstakes/social sportsbooks, not state-licensed sportsbooks. They have faced state cease-and-desists and a California class action, and Fliff has at least one withdrawal-delay complaint. Prefer licensed books and DFS apps for larger deposits.

### Cited Findings
- On Aug 15, 2025, the Arizona Department of Gaming sent cease-and-desist letters to Thrillzz, Fliff, Betty Sweeps and Pulsz. Thrillzz exited Arizona quickly. — [Sweepsy](https://www.sweepsy.com/?p=13555); [Sweepsy – Thrillzz exits AZ](https://www.sweepsy.com/news/thrillzz-leaves-arizona-following-cease-desist/)
- Secondary source (not verified against the agencies): the New York AG sent cease-and-desist letters to 26 platforms, including Fliff (June 2025), and Louisiana lists Fliff as an illegal operator. — [GamblingHarm.org](https://gamblingharm.org/is-fliff-legit-or-safe/)
- A California federal class action alleges Fliff is an illegal sportsbook. The judge sent the case to arbitration. — [LSR](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/119758/lawsuit-fliff-sued-over-illegal-sports-betting-in-california/)
- A Texas user complained of $425.74 in Fliff Cash stuck "pending" for 5 days (a single anecdote). — [Sikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/fliff-us)
- Offshore books (Lucky Rebel, BetNow, Busr) appear in affiliate "new betting sites" lists. They are not US-licensed and should be excluded. — [football-espana.net](https://www.football-espana.net/betting-sites/new-sports/)
- Many affiliate pages carry stale or misdated content. Examples: the Desert Diamond installment math, the OwnersBox $500 vs $100, and Sportsbettingdime/Action Network pages dated 2026 that describe the Missouri launch as upcoming. — [Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/kansas-city-royals-name-circa-sports-as-official-sports-betting-partner-jan-26-2026)

### Inferences
- With the sweeps/social apps (Fliff, Thrillzz, Rebet, and Novig's coin model), the risk is regulatory: a state could exit suddenly, and redemption floors and KYC delays apply. Small $5–10 entries keep that risk minimal.
- The licensed regional books (betPARX, Borgata, Betly, Desert Diamond, Bally Bet) and the major DFS apps carry the lowest counterparty risk.

### Gaps
- I did not establish Novig's regulatory status (CFTC prediction market vs sweepstakes).
- I found no 2026 enforcement actions or Reddit withdrawal threads. Reddit was not surfaced, and fetch was blocked.
